## Third-party material keeps its own license

These grants apply only to material TinyWorlds contributors are entitled to
license. They do not replace third-party copyright notices or license terms.
In particular, `vendor/`, the Godogen subtree and adapted skills retain their
upstream terms. See
[ATTRIBUTION.md](ATTRIBUTION.md), [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md),
and the source license files. A Markdown extension does not make a vendored
skill CC BY; its upstream license controls.

The C# patterns adaptation has unresolved historical source licensing. Its
local MIT description conflicts with the upstream's March 2026 LGPL change.
It is excluded from the general Apache/CC BY grants pending provenance review.
It must be resolved before a public source distribution includes that file.
Retaining a current upstream license snapshot is not proof that this settles
all historical permissions or notices.

Unidentified imported command templates, chat modes and workflow references
are likewise excluded from claims of original TinyWorlds authorship until their
provenance is established. See the outstanding rows in the notice ledger.

Generated games, uploads, images, meshes, audio, model weights, customer data,
and screenshots are not automatically licensed by this repository's license.
Their rights depend on their authors, inputs and providers. Do not include
private or unlicensed assets in public examples. Hosted service subscriptions,
API credentials and third-party model/service terms are separate from this
source code license. Project names and trademarks are not endorsements.

## Attribution and contributions

Keep upstream notices and source links when adapting skills or code. Record the
exact source revision, what changed, the applicable license, and the affected
paths. Add new sources to the attribution ledger. Attribution does not replace
permission, and a license does not establish that a skill improves outcomes.

Original contributions are submitted under the license applicable to the file
as described above, unless a separate agreement explicitly says otherwise.
Contributors must identify third-party material and preserve its terms.
